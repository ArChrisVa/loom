Join a team in Loom

This link is an invitation to a team. It works in Loom on your PC, not in the browser: copy it, and paste it there.

This address has no invitation in it. Ask your teammate for a new link: in Loom, under Settings, Team, Invite someone.

Download Loom for Windows

How to join

  1. Open Loom on your PC.
  2. Go to Settings, then Team.
  3. Paste the link under Join a team, and choose Join.
  4. Your teammate sees four digits, and so do you. When they match, they let you in.

An invitation is for one person and stops working after 15 minutes. What a team shares goes from PC to PC, sealed with a key that only its members hold. More about Loom
